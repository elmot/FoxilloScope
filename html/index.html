FoxilloScope

A dual-channel, 8 MHz sampling, 12-bit wireless digital oscilloscope. Built around low-cost hardware (ST Nucleo-G474RE & ESP32-C3). Real-time signal streaming in your browser over Wi-Fi or Bluetooth LE (BLE).
