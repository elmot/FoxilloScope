Elmot Oscilloscope Wi-Fi Configuration

The oscilloscope may use your existing WiFi network, or run its own access point (without internet access). To use the oscilloscope, either:

  1. Keep this network, acknowledge "no internet" on your device, and open the oscilloscope directly.
  2. Configure WiFi client below — then reconnect to your normal WiFi network and open the oscilloscope.
