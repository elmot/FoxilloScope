Solder Bridge Changes: Remap ST-LINK VCOM to PC4 / PC5 (USART1)

According to UM2505 §7.6.5 (Tables 10 & 11) and MB1367 schematic:

Solder Bridge Required State Connected Path Description
SB12, SB20 ON (Close / Solder) PC4 (TX) & PC5 (RX) ↔ ST-LINK VCOM Connects USART1 (PC4/PC5) to ST-LINK Virtual COM port
SB17, SB23 OFF (Open / Desolder) PA2 (TX) & PA3 (RX) ↔ ST-LINK VCOM Disconnects LPUART1 (PA2/PA3) from ST-LINK Virtual COM port
SB13, SB19 OFF (Open / Desolder) PC4 (TX) & PC5 (RX) ↔ ARDUINO D1/D0 Disconnects USART1 (PC4/PC5) from ARDUINO D1/D0 & Morpho CN10-35/37
SB18, SB22 ON (Close / Solder) PA2 (TX) & PA3 (RX) ↔ ARDUINO D1/D0 Connects LPUART1 (PA2/PA3) to ARDUINO D1/D0 & Morpho CN10-35/37
